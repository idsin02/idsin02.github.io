01 — operator profile

Security research, grounded in practice.

I'm Dhruv Singh, a threat hunter based in Pune, India, publishing security work as DHRUxMAN. I work in Cyber Threat Intelligence & Hunting at Tata Consultancy Services. There I turn threat intelligence into hunts, run dark web and digital risk monitoring, and build hypothesis-driven hunts across endpoint, identity, network, and cloud telemetry.

I start with the adversary. I break down the vulnerability or TTP, engineer the detection, then validate it against real telemetry. I maintain multi-format detection content for actively exploited CVEs and hunt across Sysmon, Microsoft Sentinel, Defender XDR (KQL), and CrowdStrike Falcon (CQL).

Before this, I completed a VAPT internship at Qseap Infotech and was acknowledged in NASA's Hall of Fame on Bugcrowd for responsible disclosure. My research and tooling are public, so you can check them yourself.
